## German inspection and invoicing boundaries

For the work-equipment inspections within §14 BetrSichV, the record must capture the inspection type, scope, outcome and qualified signatory. The retention requirement extends at least to the next inspection. Qualifying electronic transmission requires an electronic signature. Map the applicable inspection regime and qualified person's review before releasing a protocol. [BetrSichV §14(7)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrsichv_2015/__14.html) · [DGUV guidance on recurring inspections](https://publikationen.dguv.de/widgets/pdf/download/article/2879)

The German Finance Ministry identifies XRechnung and eligible ZUGFeRD profiles as structured invoice formats. A PDF or XML upload passing the scanner establishes file handling only. Financial release work still needs format/profile validation, retention of the structured original, auditable review and professional verification of scope and transition dates for each company. [BMF e-invoicing FAQ](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/FAQ/e-rechnung.html)
